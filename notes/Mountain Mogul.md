# Mountain Mogul

A ski resort tycoon on real mountains. The player designs, builds, and runs a resort on terrain imported from real elevation data, from a local hill to a big international peak. The core loop balances running the mountain against the economy.

## Pillars

- **Real geography.** If it exists, you can ski it. The terrain is real topography ([[Terrain Import]]).
- **A living mountain.** Hundreds to thousands of individual guests choose where to go from their skill, energy, and goals ([[GOAP]], [[Skiing]]).
- **The whole resort.** The player runs everything from the parking lot to the penthouse, not just lift tickets ([[Amenities]], [[Finance]]; real estate is in [[Next Steps]]).

The original design doc had a fourth pillar, "eternal winter": no time of day, no seasons. The game has moved past it: [[Calendar]] runs days, nights, and the whole year, and the [[Vision]] is built around the seasons.

[[Vision]] tells the story of the next iteration, from an empty mountain to a resort with a gondola. [[Next Steps]] is the list of what to build.
