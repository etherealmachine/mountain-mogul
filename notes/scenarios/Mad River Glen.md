# Mad River Glen

Northern Vermont. A cooperative-owned, skiers-only hill with a famous single chair and as little grooming as it can get away with. Its motto: "Ski It If You Can." Step 4 of the [[Scenario Campaign]].

**Premise.** The co-op wants to grow, but its members will walk if it turns into a mega-resort.

**Teaches.** Balancing growth against character. Season pass holders and regulars matter more than day visitors; crowds, price rises, and too much new building cost goodwill ([[Demand]], [[Finance]]).

**Goals (draft).** Grow revenue while keeping a locals score above a threshold; keep the pass-holder share up.

**Rules.** Skiers only. Locals' resort score on.

**Needs.**

- A locals score ([[Scenario Goals and Rules]])
- Guests remembering their last visit, and regulars (in [[Next Steps]])
- Crowding that guests notice beyond lift lines
