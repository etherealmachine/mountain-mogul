# Alta

Little Cottonwood Canyon, Utah. Huge snowfall, steep slide paths above the resort and the only road in. After big storms the canyon road closes for avalanche control, and guests can be ordered to stay indoors ("interlodge"). Step 5 of the [[Scenario Campaign]].

**Premise.** The snow is the draw and the danger. Keep guests safe and the lifts turning through storm cycles.

**Teaches.** Reading avalanche risk and acting before a slide: control work, closing runs, keeping patrol close ([[Avalanche]], [[Ski Patrol]], [[Trails]]). A closed road means no arrivals that day ([[Parking and Roads]], [[Demand]]).

**Goals (draft).** A season with no guests caught in a slide; open within a set time after each storm.

**Rules.** Skiers only. Heavy-snow climate. The canyon road closes after big storms.

**Needs.**

- Avalanche control, closures, and a risk overlay (in [[Next Steps]])
- Road closures that stop arrivals
- Trail closures (in [[Next Steps]])
