# Killington

Central Vermont. The "Beast of the East," famous for racing to open first in the fall and staying open into late spring, mostly on machine-made snow. Step 3 of the [[Scenario Campaign]].

**Premise.** Natural snow is unreliable. Make your own and stretch the season at both ends.

**Teaches.** [[Snowmaking]] on cold nights, laying a deep base early so it lasts ([[Snow]], [[Weather]]), and the costs of running when few guests come ([[Finance]]).

**Goals (draft).** Open by a date in November; stay open past a date in May; finish the season in the black.

**Rules.** An East Coast climate: thaws, rain, and less natural snow.

**Needs.**

- A per-scenario climate ([[Scenario Goals and Rules]])
- Fast-forward to a freezing night or a base depth ([[Calendar]])
- Possibly a snowmaking water budget
