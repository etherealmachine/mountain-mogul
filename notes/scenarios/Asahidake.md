# Asahidake

Daisetsuzan, Hokkaido. One ropeway up a steaming volcano, light dry powder nearly every day, and no grooming at all. Step 6 of the [[Scenario Campaign]].

**Premise.** Guests fly in for powder and trees, not corduroy. Run a resort where the snow is never groomed.

**Teaches.** Managing powder and glades instead of groomed runs. Thinning trees into skiable glades, spreading guests out so the powder isn't skied off by ten, and attracting expert, powder-hunting guests ([[Trees]], [[Snow]], [[Skiing]]).

**Goals (draft).** A high rating from powder-loving guests; a set number of glades opened without stripping the forest.

**Rules.** No grooming. A Hokkaido climate with light snow almost every day.

**Needs.**

- No-grooming rule and per-scenario climate ([[Scenario Goals and Rules]])
- Guest goals like hunting powder (in [[Next Steps]])
- [[Stored Trees]], so glading is precise and guests react to real trees
