# Zermatt

Valais, Switzerland. A car-free village under the Matterhorn, reached by train, with lifts climbing to a glacier. Step 7 of the [[Scenario Campaign]].

**Premise.** No cars and no parking. Everyone arrives by train into the village and has to get up the mountain from there.

**Teaches.** Moving guests from a station through a village to the lifts, with no parking lot to spread them out. Big lifts out of town, long gondolas to high terrain ([[Lifts]], [[Pathfinding]]).

**Goals (draft).** A set number of guests a day with no parking; a lift to the glacier.

**Rules.** No cars. Alpine climate, very high top elevation.

**Needs.**

- Trains as the way guests arrive (in [[Next Steps]])
- Footpaths through a village (in [[Next Steps]])
- No-cars rule ([[Scenario Goals and Rules]])
- Glaciers, and employee housing for staff who can't drive in (in [[Next Steps]])
