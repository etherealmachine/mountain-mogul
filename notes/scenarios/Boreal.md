# Boreal

Donner Pass, California. A small, beginner-friendly hill right off the interstate. The tutorial. Step 1 of the [[Scenario Campaign]].

**Goals (draft).** Open the resort; reach a set number of guests a day; hold a decent rating through a weekend.
