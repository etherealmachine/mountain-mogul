# Boreal

Donner Pass, California. A small, beginner-friendly hill right off the interstate. The tutorial. Step 1 of the [[Scenario Campaign]].

**Premise.** An empty base area beside the highway and enough money for one lift. Open before the holidays.

**Teaches.** A lift ([[Lifts]]), a few runs ([[Trails]]), a lodge with tickets and food ([[Amenities]]), opening the resort ([[Calendar]]), and reading the rating ([[Demand]]).

**Goals.** Required: three lifts open at the end of a day, and one day with 1,000 or more guests that ends with the day's rating at 3.0 stars or better (the user, 2026-10-10; it was a week at 70% before the rating moved to stars and the season to 30 days). Bonus: all of them within season one. No deadline.

**Rules.** None. Everything is simple and forgiving.

**Needs.** Playable as `boreal.save`, named Boreal with its description and marked as the tutorial ([[Scenario Metadata]]). Still needs tutorial goals ([[Scenario Goals and Rules]]).

**Build order and money**: [[Boreal Tutorial]].

**Lifts.** The Goals Test save builds three real Boreal lifts, all from the base, in an obvious order: California Cruiser (the beginner lift), Accelerator (the main lift, a high-speed quad), then 49er. The save still calls them Lift4, Lift1 and Lift3. Costs and income in [[Economy Balance]].
