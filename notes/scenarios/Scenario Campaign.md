# Scenario Campaign

A run of real resorts in rising difficulty. Each one adds a feature or a twist, so finishing the campaign teaches the whole game. Uses [[Scenario Metadata]] for names and descriptions and [[Scenario Goals and Rules]] for objectives and constraints.

## Order

1. [[Boreal]]: the basics on a small hill (the tutorial)
2. [[Kirkwood]]: beginners and experts on one mountain
3. [[Killington]]: open first, close last, on machine-made snow
4. [[Mad River Glen]]: grow without losing the locals
5. [[Alta]]: avalanches and a canyon road that closes
6. [[Asahidake]]: a powder mountain with no grooming
7. [[Zermatt]]: no cars; everyone arrives by train
8. [[Palisades Tahoe]]: join two resorts with one gondola

Every scenario note lists the features it needs. Most aren't built yet, so the campaign also works as a rough order for [[Next Steps]].

## More ideas

Not ranked or written up yet:

- **Portillo, Chile**: one hotel, and every guest stays in it; a June–October season; slingshot surface lifts. Needs hotels, a southern-hemisphere calendar, surface lifts.
- **Turnaround**: a struggling hill with old lifts, debt, and a bad rating. Fix it before the bank does. Needs lift wear and maintenance.
- **Whistler Blackcomb**: two mountains joined at the top by the Peak 2 Peak gondola. An alternative finale to [[Palisades Tahoe]].
- **Revelstoke or a BC cat-skiing lodge**: premium guests, cat and heli skiing, huge vertical.
- **Big Sky**: a single aerial tram to a summit only experts can ski down.
