# Rental Shop

A lodge counter where arriving guests pick up skis before they can ride. Part of [[Amenities]]. A lodge service (`ServiceRentals`, $30,000 a tile, $120 a day to run) with its own facade windows and a rental price in the building popup (default $40).

In the season story it sits on the walk from parking to [[Tickets]] to the lift. The counter's door is wider than a lounge door, and the morning rush is the first staffing puzzle: one counter and one employee is a long line, and a second counter or a second employee is the fix. Guests should not carry skis through the lodge; a ski rack at the snow is part of the same beat.

It gates getting on the mountain at all, alongside [[Tickets]]. Guests roll whether they came without skis each visit: half of beginners, 15% of intermediates, 5% of advanced, never a pass holder; they bring the rental money on top of their budget. Until they rent they can't join a lift line, so their first stop is the shop (15 clock minutes, 3 guests a tile at once, a line at the door when full). A resort with no rental shop turns them round in the car park ("nowhere to rent skis", departing "Couldn't rent skis"), and one that charges far more than they expect does the same ([[Service Improvements]] step 3).

Still story only: staff, a ski rack at the snow, and returning the skis.

Spec: [[Vision]], season 2.
