# Rental Shop

A lodge counter where arriving guests pick up skis before they can ride. Part of [[Amenities]]. Not a service in the building code yet.

In the season story it sits on the walk from parking to [[Tickets]] to the lift. The counter's door is wider than a lounge door, and the morning rush is the first staffing puzzle: one counter and one employee is a long line, and a second counter or a second employee is the fix. Guests should not carry skis through the lodge; a ski rack at the snow is part of the same beat.

No guest stat points here yet. The thing it would gate is getting on the mountain at all, alongside [[Tickets]].

Spec: [[Vision]], season 2.
