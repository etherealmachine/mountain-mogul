# Condos

Units sold to owners. Part of [[Amenities]]; the middle of lodging in [[Services]] (Overnight).

Between a [[Hotel]]'s ongoing revenue and [[Houses]]' one-off land sale: a burst of income when units sell, owners who come back as regular guests, and units that can go into a rental pool between owners' visits for a share of nightly revenue.
