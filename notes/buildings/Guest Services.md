# Guest Services

A guest services desk, with the season pass office. Part of [[Amenities]]; a convenience service in [[Services]].

The desk answers questions, keeps lost property, and hands out maps: mostly flavour, with a small boost. The pass office is the part with teeth: season pass holders who have one get their pass sorted faster and with better service than at the day-ticket [[Tickets]] window, which matters at the morning rush.
