# Demo Shop

A shop that lends performance skis to try for the day. Part of [[Amenities]]; one of the gear services in [[Services]].

Where the [[Rental Shop]] gates beginners who came without skis, a demo shop is a want for confident skiers: some intermediate and advanced guests arrive wanting to try new skis. Pricier than a rental, and a good day on demo skis scores a little better. It can share a building with the rental shop.
