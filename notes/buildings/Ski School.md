# Ski School

Lessons, from group classes to private instruction. Part of [[Amenities]]; the learning service in [[Services]].

Its need, a **lesson**, is rolled for beginners (and some intermediates). A lesson raises a guest's skill, so beginners grow into blue runs over their visits and come back more often: the biggest lever on progression and retention among the day services. It needs gentle terrain or a magic carpet near the school, and instructors (staff). Large enough that it may get a plan of its own.
