# Ski School

Lessons, from group classes to private instruction. Part of [[Amenities]]; the learning service in [[Services]].

Built (see [[Families]], How it works): a service room with a desk and a meeting spot, a lesson price and an instructor headcount; instructors are staff who take classes of up to eight out for two hours, and each lesson raises a guest's skill for good, less each time.

Its need, a **lesson**, is rolled for beginners (and some intermediates). A lesson raises a guest's skill, so beginners grow into blue runs over their visits and come back more often: the biggest lever on progression and retention among the day services. Kids take lessons too: a family goes to the desk together, only the kid signs up, and a parent collects them afterwards. Gentle terrain or a magic carpet ([[Surface Lifts]]) near the school helps.
