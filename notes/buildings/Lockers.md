# Lockers

Lockers or a boot room at the base. Part of [[Amenities]]; one of the convenience services in [[Services]].

Guests who store their bags and shoes walk lighter between the car park and the snow; a small boost on arrival and leaving, for a small fee. Ties into footpaths and ski racks ([[Pathfinding]], [[Next Steps]] Base area).
