# Shuttles and Parking

Shuttles from far lots, and parking tiers (close-in premium parking). Part of [[Amenities]]; a convenience service in [[Services]].

They're transit ([[Transit]], [[Parking and Roads]]), but they can also be services a guest uses (`UseService`): a ride from a far lot meets the walk from the car, and premium parking sells the short walk for a price.
