# Retail Shop

A shop selling gloves, goggles, hats, and souvenirs. Part of [[Amenities]]; one of the gear services in [[Services]].

Its need, **forgot something**, is rolled at arrival and grows on cold days (no gloves at −15 °C). Mostly impulse revenue for the resort, and a guest who couldn't buy what they forgot is colder and crosser. Sits well beside the [[Rental Shop]].
