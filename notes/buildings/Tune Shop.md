# Tune Shop

A bench that waxes and sharpens skis. Part of [[Amenities]]; one of the gear services in [[Services]].

Its need, a **tune**, comes from skiing: icy days and falls dull edges, and a guest who notices their skis sliding out wants them sharpened. Low priority next to the other gear services.
