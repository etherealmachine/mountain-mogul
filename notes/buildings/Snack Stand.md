# Snack Stand

A one-tile kiosk (1×1, no seats) that sells a quick bite and a drink. Part of [[Amenities]]; one of the food services in [[Services]].

It meets a little [[Hunger]] and [[Thirst]], less than a meal, in about five clock minutes and at a low price. With no seats it's no rest stop. Its point is placement: a stand fits where a lodge doesn't, at a lift top, mid-mountain, or by a lift line, so guests refuel without skiing down to the base. Building on the mountain costs more with elevation and to keep supplied ([[Services]]: building anywhere).

A [[Food Court]] smaller than its minimum size is a snack stand.
