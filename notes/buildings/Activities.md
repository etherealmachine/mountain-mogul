# Activities

Things to do besides skiing: a tubing hill, sledding, snowshoe and nordic trails, an ice rink, scenic gondola rides, snowmobile tours, a terrain park. Part of [[Amenities]]; the activity group in [[Services]].

They serve guests who don't ski, and skiers taking a break. Most need non-skiing guests first ([[Next Steps]], Scenarios and campaign); the terrain park is the exception, since it's terrain and a taste.
