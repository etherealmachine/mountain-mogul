# Budget Lodging

A hostel or motel, possibly off the slope. Part of [[Amenities]]; lodging in [[Services]] (Overnight).

Cheap and plain: brings younger skiers on smaller budgets who wouldn't stay at a [[Hotel]]. RV and camping lots are its cheapest form, a parking lot with hookups.
