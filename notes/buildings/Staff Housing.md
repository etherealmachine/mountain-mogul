# Staff Housing

Beds for the resort's employees. Part of [[Amenities]]; a staff service in [[Services]] (Staff services).

Not for guests: without housing, a resort far from town can't hire enough staff, so it caps how many services and lifts can run. Staff aren't guests and won't share guests' stats and services; they'll likely reuse the planner the way [[Transit]] and logistics might.
