# Houses

Land sold for houses. Part of [[Amenities]]; the sell-the-land end of lodging in [[Services]] (Overnight).

The most money up front and the least after: the resort sells land and gives up nightly revenue. Owners become regular guests. Uses the most land per guest, so it trades against space for lifts, parking, and a village.
