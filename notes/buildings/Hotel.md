# Hotel

Rooms by the night, run by the resort. Part of [[Amenities]]; the resort-run end of lodging in [[Services]] (Overnight).

The trade-off with lodging is ongoing revenue against a lump sum: a hotel earns every night it's full, and costs staff and upkeep every day, where [[Condos]] and [[Houses]] sell once. Its guests stay several nights and ski several days, so they don't drive in each morning, and they use the evening services. A stay is a service like any other: the room's quality, price, and location (slopeside, ski-in/ski-out, views) score on their ledger, and the resort's rating stays the daily one.

On-site lodging raises demand: beds on the mountain bring destination guests from farther away, so a really big resort needs a village.
