# Night Skiing

Lit trails and lifts in the evening. Part of [[Amenities]]; an evening service in [[Services]] (Overnight), and a lift service.

The resort stays open past dark on chosen trails and lifts and pays to run the lights, for an evening session that overnight guests and locals after work come for. Operating hours and the day-night cycle exist; trail lights don't. Planned in [[Night Sessions]].
