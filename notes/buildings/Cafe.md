# Café

A small coffee shop (one or two tiles). Part of [[Amenities]]; one of the food services in [[Services]].

It meets a new rolled need, **coffee**: some guests arrive wanting one, most urgently on arrival, fading by late morning, so a café by the car park or the ticket window catches the morning rush. A coffee and a pastry also meet a little [[Hunger]] and [[Thirst]], and a hot drink warms a chilled guest a little (the [[Lounge]]'s fire stays the real warm-up). Cheap and quick, with a few seats.
