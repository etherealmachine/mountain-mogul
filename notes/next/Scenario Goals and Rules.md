# Scenario Goals and Rules

What makes one scenario play differently from another, beyond the map. Builds on [[Scenario Metadata]]. Listed in [[Next Steps]].

## Goals

Each scenario lists objectives, checked as the game runs and shown in a goals panel:

- a resort rating held for a stretch of days ([[Demand]])
- guests per day, or season passes sold
- cash on hand, or the credit line paid off ([[Finance]])
- something built: a lift type, a lift between two named points, a lodge with a food court
- a deadline: a date, or the end of season N ([[Calendar]])

Meeting them wins the scenario. Bankruptcy, or missing a deadline, loses it.

## Rules

Constraints that change how the mountain works, set per scenario:

- **No grooming.** Snowcats and sheds can't be built, and guests judge the resort on powder instead of corduroy. See [[Grooming]].
- **No cars.** No roads or parking lots; guests arrive by train ([[Parking and Roads]]).
- **Skiers only.** No snowboarders in the catchment. Snowboarders are already rolled ([[Guest Types]]), but they don't behave differently yet.
- **Snowmaking limits**: none allowed, or a water budget ([[Snowmaking]]).
- **Locals' resort.** A score for how much the resort still feels local, which drops with crowding, high prices, and too few pass holders.
- **Climate.** Each scenario picks its own monthly [[Weather]] profiles, ideally fitted to real-world climate data for the place: monthly snowfall, temperatures, and storm frequency. Today there is one set for every map. A southern-hemisphere resort also needs the season to run June to October.

## Progression

Scenarios unlock in order. Each one introduces a feature or two the earlier ones didn't need, so the campaign doubles as the tutorial for the whole game. [[Scenario Campaign]] lays out that order.

## Open questions

- Are goals all-or-nothing, or bronze, silver, gold?
- Can the player keep playing after winning, as a sandbox?
- Do rules live as named switches, or as limits on the build menu (the cat shed just isn't offered)?
