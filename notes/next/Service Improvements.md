# Service Improvements

Give each base-area service a job of its own, make price, quality, and capacity matter to guests, and add the next service types. The services work ([[Building Services]], the satisfaction ledger in [[Satisfaction]]); what's missing is a reason to build one over another, or a better one over a cheaper one. Part of [[Amenities]]. Waiting on the user's decisions before it's ranked (Open questions).

## Why

Read on 2026-10-07, after "Fix how services work" closed. What each service does today:

| Service | Stats | Score (ledger) |
|---|---|---|
| [[Food Court]] | a meal fills hunger and thirst; its seats are a rest; it pours drinks | meal +0.05, drink +0.04, rest +0.03 |
| [[Bar]] | a drink fills thirst | +0.04, the same as a food-court drink |
| [[Lounge]] | a rest fills energy and patience | +0.03 |
| [[Tickets]] | day tickets and passes | none |
| [[Ski Patrol]] | rescues | fast +0.06, slow −0.08 |
| Garage | snowcats and snowmobiles | none |

- **A bar is a food court that only pours drinks**: since food courts pour them too, it has no reason to exist.
- **Every building is the same** but for price, and price only decides whether a guest can afford it, not how they feel about it.
- **No capacity at the door** except food-court seats: no lines at a busy lodge, and no complaint about one.
- **Few service types**: a [[Rental Shop]] plan exists; ski school and lockers are one-line ideas.

## The ideas

1. **A job for each service.** First thoughts, pending the user (Open questions):
   - Bar: après-ski, a late-afternoon stop for guests who've had a good day, with a bonus that grows with their day; a reason to stay past closing and spend, and a good end to the day.
   - Lounge: warming up, worth more on cold days.
   - Food court: lunch, with drinks alongside.
2. **Price against value.** A guest weighs a price against their budget and the building's quality: "good value" adds to their score, "overpriced" takes from it, and far overpriced turns them away.
3. **Quality.** Each service building has a quality (size, finish or style, views of the slopes) that scales what its services give and that guests weigh when choosing where to go. Folds in **Amenity quality and views** ([[Next Steps]], Base area).
4. **Capacity and lines.** Each tile serves guests at a rate; lines form at the door, waiting drains patience, and long waits get a thought. Folds in **Door queues and service rates**.
5. **New service types**, in order: the [[Rental Shop]] (beginners want it), ski school, lockers.

## Open questions

The user is thinking these over (2026-10-07):

- **What role bars play.** Après-ski is one answer; others are possible.
- **How quality is raised**: whether improving a building means rebuilding it, upgrading it in place (the way lifts upgrade on the same line), or something else.
- **What rental shops do**: see [[Rental Shop]].
- Which comes first once those are settled; service jobs and value (ideas 1–2) give every service a reason to exist, so they're the likely start.
