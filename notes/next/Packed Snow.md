# Packed Snow

Snow that people on foot pack down, and that staff keep clear: walkers slow in deep or rough snow, and lift sweepers keep the ground round each station packed and passable. Listed in [[Next Steps]]; extends [[Lifts]] and [[Pathfinding]].

## What's there today

- Walking speed is the same on any snow; only a footpath changes it (twice as fast).
- Nothing grooms around lift stations: cats groom trail passes ([[Grooming]]), so station aprons and lift-line areas get fresh snow and moguls like any ungroomed ground, and nothing reacts to it.
- Every lift has two operators and an optional line attendant. There are no sweepers.

## The idea

1. **Walkers slow in rough snow.** Deep fresh snow, moguls and crust slow anyone on foot. Packed or groomed snow and footpaths don't.
2. **Feet pack snow.** Guests walking and standing in line pack the cells under them over the day, like grooming but by traffic.
3. **Sweepers.** An optional sweeper at a lift's top and one at its bottom keep the area round each station packed and clear through the day, like a small cat that never leaves. Without one, a busy base turns rough after a snowfall, which slows the line and the walk to it. A rough top apron could raise unload falls ([[Lift Unloading]]).

## Open questions

- Should packing be its own cell value, or reuse grooming (a cell walked flat counts as groomed)?
- Do sweepers also clear footpaths near the lift, or only the station area?
