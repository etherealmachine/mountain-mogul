# Lift Maintenance

Maintenance and breakdowns as a risk the player takes on: lifts wear, and the player can run them harder than they should, by ignoring a wind hold or skimping on maintenance, and pay for it in breakdowns or, at worst, a failure. Grows step 2 (Breakdowns) of [[Lift Operations]]; touches [[Lifts]], [[Finance]], [[Ski Patrol]] and [[Moments]].

## The idea

From the user, 2026-10-14:

- **Maintenance costs money and buys reliability.** Paying for it (a contract, a mechanic, scheduled downtime) keeps wear and breakdowns down; not paying lets them climb.
- **Running in the wind is a gamble.** The player can override a wind hold ([[Lift Operations]], Built: wind, holds and test runs) and keep a lift loading past its limit, for the guests and the money, at a rising risk of a breakdown, or a failure.
- **Breakdowns and failures.** A breakdown stops the lift with riders on it: short ones restart, long ones need an evacuation by patrol. A failure is the worst case (a long closure, a big repair bill, a hit to the rating), the price of pushing a worn lift in a gale.

## Open questions

Not worked out yet (the user): how it shows in the UI and which features it needs. Among them:

- What maintenance is to the player: a per-lift slider or level, a resort-wide contract, mechanics on staff, scheduled closed days, or a mix.
- How wear shows: a condition bar in the lift popup, a warning in the feed, sounds and stops on the line.
- How overriding a wind hold works: a button on the held lift, how long it lasts, what the popup says about the risk.
- What a failure is in play: how rare, how long, what it costs, whether guests get hurt or only stranded, and whether it can end a scenario.
- Whether age and type matter (an old fixed-grip double against a new detachable).
