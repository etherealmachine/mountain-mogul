# Boreal Playtest

The user's notes from playing the rebuilt [[Boreal Tutorial]] start, 2026-10-11, ranked. Each item keeps the user's words in quotes, then says what it likely means and where it belongs. Bugs get diagnosed before they're fixed. Listed in [[Next Steps]]; feeds [[Demo]].

## Ranked

The order puts first what blocks playing the tutorial or reading its results, then what makes the skiing look wrong, then polish.

1. Done: **Start paused.** "start paused". A new game or a loaded save opens paused; testbeds and `-screenshot` runs still start running.
2. Done: **Pause between days.** "pause between days". The game pauses at midnight behind the day report, which gains "Start the next day" and a "Pause every night" toggle (a setting, on by default); with the report turned off, a toast says it's paused for the night.
3. Done: **The star rating moves during the day.** "star rating only moves after midnight". Once anyone has left today, the top bar shows today's average so far ("2.7 today"), with a tick on the bar at the last full day's rating; before then it shows the last full day's "stars" as before. The rating the goals check is still set at rollover.
4. Done: **Cars left in the lot overnight** (bug). "cars in the parking lot overnight". Diagnosed: a car leaves only with its whole carload, and one member never left: either a skier circling in a pocket of terrain (only followers had a stuck-run timeout) or a follower frozen since morning (a full line sent them to wait for the leader in a way that left their step unfinished, so they were never re-planned and the 10-minute wait limit never ran). Closing only acted at the end of a step, so neither ever went home; on the Goals Test 1 → 47 → 48 cars stayed overnight, holding their stalls, over three days. Fixed: a guest waiting for their group or on a run gone on too long (any guest, `descentStuck`) is always re-checked; after closing, anyone not on a run, in a line, indoors, resting, down, or off for après goes home at once (`dueHome`); a guest mid-run finishes it and goes home by the trails. Re-measured: 0 cars and 0 guests at 02:00 three nights running, 80% gone by 17:00, the last car out by about 19:30.
5. Done: **Skier stuck taking skis on and off** (bug). "stuck skier following another, putting on then taking off skis". Diagnosed: a group follower kept following their leader's line after the leader went into a lodge, stepped onto the lodge's bare floor (skis off at once), walked their own way back to the snow, put skis on (15 s), and turned back toward the leader, for the whole visit. What made it hard to see: skis off is instant, so they were always caught putting skis on, on snow, for no visible reason. Fixed: a follower drops the line once the leader is off the snow (indoors, waiting, or skis off), and never follows onto ground they'd have to walk. Also fixed on the way: guests crossing patchy bare ground put skis on for every patch (now only when the next 15 m toward their next waypoint is snow, `bareAhead`); a walk to a lift carries the skis, which go on in the line; guests in a line or on a chair keep their skis on over bare ground, and nobody rides up carrying them (`clipInToRide`; about 300 a day did).
6. Done: **Snow over fresh grooming** (bug). "groomed but snow fell overnight to wipe it out (wind slab, but grooming texture still shows?)". Diagnosed: cats groomed in the evening, then the midnight rollover played the new day's weather and, with 6-day months, several real days besides; runs opened on that weather (on the Goals Test, about 1% groomed at opening). Only snowfall and melt lowered a cell's grooming, and only a full day's burial cleared the corduroy map, so a wind slab or crust kept drawing corduroy. Fixed: cats work from the rollover to half an hour before opening, after the weather (`catsOnShift`), so runs open 99.8% groomed; every surface change ungrooms a cell and clears its stamps (`Terrain.Ungroom`); a groomed top melting onto the base keeps its grooming. Also closes Fresh corduroy turns to crust overnight. Only a light snowfall (under about 1 cm of water) still shows corduroy, and the cats re-groom after it.
7. Done: **Patchy grooming.** "kind of a crap grooming job - 88%, plenty of time, missing spots". Diagnosed: cats keep 3 m from every trunk (by design since 2026-10-08), and the tutorial's runs still have trees in them (2,096 of 9,156 trail cells); those cells counted in the run's Groom %, which reproduced 86–88%. Edges, towers and steepness weren't a factor. Fixed: the run popup leaves cells cats can't reach out of the Groom % and says "N% under trees: cats won't groom" (`GroomReach`); clearing the trees is the player's fix. Also found: one cat can't groom a big network in a night (Boreal's needs about 16 hours); a cut-off pass now resumes the next night (`holdRoute`), so far runs get groomed on alternate nights.
8. Done (first part): **Beginners care about grooming.** "beginners could probably want more grooming and complain a bit more about it missing". A beginner who loves corduroy (groomed taste 0.3 or more, most Cruisers) after a run that's less than 40% groomed thinks "I wish they'd groom <run>" (an annoyance, `ThoughtNotGroomed`), and that run can't be a great run (`runGroomedWanted`, `sim/runs.go`). Patrol's half of [[Boreal Tutorial]] change 4 is still open.
9. **Lift lines.** Three notes about the same thing:
   - "skiers need to path to the back of the line": arriving guests walk through or cut in instead of going round to the end of the line.
   - "line out the parking lot looks bad - attendant can auto-manage line, need merging lines with multiple lanes and groups + single rider and ski patrol": a long line runs straight out into the parking lot. The line attendant should lay it out (switchbacks, like the hand-built mazes in [[Rope Lines]]), with lanes that merge at the load: groups, a singles lane, and a patrol lane.
   - "spread waiting groups out at the top of the lift": guests unloaded at the top stand in a clump; spread them out where they gather.

   The first is a bug and comes first; the attendant-managed line is a design to plan with the user ([[Lifts]], [[Lift Operations]], [[Rope Lines]]).

   Diagnosed (none of Boreal's lifts use lanes, so this is the single-file line): guests joined a line on reaching the lift base (within 8 m), which is its front, were given the last place, and walked straight back through everyone to it (on the Goals Test, 823 of 1,124 joins at Lift4 passed about 40 queuers); and the line ran straight downhill with no limit (Lift4's reached 166 m, into the lot). Built (the back-of-line fix, and a stopgap for the lot): near the base counts only while the line is short, so guests join at its back; arrivals head round the line to its back from outside it (`QueueApproachXZ`), and walkers route to the back; the line folds into switchback lanes 1.5 m apart, at most 20 places (40 m) a lane and before the first lot or blocked cell, on the side with more room (`UpdateQueueFolds`, hourly; `internal/world/lift_queue_fold.go`). Waiting at the top: every member of a group stopped where the scripted unload ends (median 4–5 others within 0.5 m). Built: a guest waiting for their group at a lift top walks to a hold spot about 4 m out from the unload, away from the line; their group fans out 1.5 m apart, other groups 3 m further out (`holdSpot`, `internal/sim/groups.go`): the median waiter has 0 others within 0.5 m, the worst 11 (from 16), and those left are guests changing skis. The fold's lanes after the first start 6 m out, clear of the station's legs and hut, on the side away from the hut, and arrivals walk along the outside of the line to its back before stepping in. Re-measured on the Goals Test: Lift4's arrivals passed 615 queuers on the way to their place, from 23,558; no place in a lot. The rest is planned as [[Lift Lines]] (2026-10-12): a loose crowd with no attendant; with one, an automatic rope maze with groups, singles and patrol lanes.
10. Started: **Guests' thoughts.** "need better events - skier thoughts, etc". First step built: the event feed says "Guests are saying \"…\" (n of m today)" when a new complaint becomes the most common reason for today's reviews below 3★ (checked each clock hour, at least 5 reviews; `logGuestsSay`), and the Reviews tab shows today so far once anyone has left today. The rest, and what "etc" covers, to plan with the user ([[Readable Complaints]], [[Event Feed]], [[Moments]]).
11. Done (the cause): **Slower speeds.** "slow down every speed - seems to only affect when resort is closed". Diagnosed: a closed resort with nobody about counted as quiet all day, not just at night, so the whole day passed in the 2 s quiet skip at every speed button; the tutorial starts closed, so every button looked the same and far too fast. Fixed: only nights are quiet (`quietNow`); a closed day runs at the chosen pace. The presets themselves (2,560, 160, 80 s a day) are unchanged: ask the user whether they still want them slower now ([[Season Calendar]]).
12. Done: **Skiers pass under lift stations** (bug). "don't ski under the station footprint". Diagnosed: stations were skier obstacles only as eight points (legs and hut), with the terminal between them open; getting off glided every rider 5 m straight through the top station; and every top run started at the bullwheel, so skiers turned back through the station to reach it (on the Goals Test, about 2,000 skiing guest-seconds a day under Lift1's top terminal). What made it hard to see: the scripted unload and the turn back both look like normal skiing, and the steering overlay never drew stations. Fixed: station footprints as boxes from the model (`internal/world/lift_station_footprint.go`); riders stand up under the hood front, peel sideways and come round beside the hut onto the apron before skiing off (straight downhill in front dropped beginners onto Lift1's 25–30° pitch: falls near tops went up fourfold); top runs start beside the station (`TopRunEnd`); skiers steer clear of top terminals and huts, walkers too, and group hold spots stay out. Base terminals stay open for the loading lane. Lift1's top: skiing inside 2,012 → 1 guest-seconds a day, falls within 30 m of a top 22–25 → 10–18.
13. Done: **Walking with skis on** (bug). "walking skier still has skis on". Diagnosed: the drawing was right; the guests had skis on. Skis went on whenever a guest stood on snow, even on a walk to a building or car, so walkers clipped in on every patch of snow on the way to the door (15 s each time), and came off instantly on bare ground: on the Goals Test about 2 million guest-ticks a day of walking to buildings and cars on skis. Guests also arrived with skis on, from before ski racks. Fixed: on a walk to a building, ticket office or car, skis come off (even on snow) and stay off (`onWalkErrand`, `maybeStartSkiTransition`); renters never put on skis they don't have; guests arrive carrying skis and put them on at the snow. A walk to a lift still puts them on at the snow and shuffles to the line.
14. Done: **Follow the path.** "skiers should try to follow the path", read as walkers keeping to footpaths. Diagnosed: the walking search did route along paths, but cutting the route down to its corners replaced it with a straight line whenever either end (a car or a door) was off the path, which is nearly every walk; it only kept to a path when both ends of a cut were on it. A probe on Boreal's terrain found 0% of an L-shaped path walked with both ends 15 m off it. Fixed: a corner cut is kept only if walking it is no slower than the route it replaces, with path cells at twice the speed (`footShortcutOK`, in `FindPath`'s corners and in `planSkiRoute` and `routeTarget`'s skip-ahead). What made it hard to see: the route was right; the smoothing after it threw it away. Paths still lose to a much shorter walk over snow, by design. If "the path" meant trails, ask the user.
15. **Loading animation.** "getting on lift animation needs work". The boarding motion onto the chair. Graphics polish for [[Demo]] step 10; ask the user what looked wrong.
