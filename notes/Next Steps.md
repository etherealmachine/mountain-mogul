# Next Steps

New features only, a line each, to say yes or no to. A feature big enough to need its own design gets a plan note in `notes/next/` and a link here. When something ships, take it off this list and add it to today's line in the [[Changelog]]. Known bugs are in [[Bugs]]; what's left over on a built feature (tuning, performance, polish, extensions) lives in that feature's own note.

*Needs* is what has to exist first; *For* names the scenarios that want it, so the [[Scenario Campaign]] doubles as a rough order. To prioritize something, move it up into Priority.

## Priority

Nothing ranked.

## Lifts

- [[Lift Maintenance]]: breakdowns, paying for maintenance, and overriding wind holds at a risk.
- **Riding lifts down**: beginners stuck at a top, tired guests at the end of the day, a bare lower mountain in spring; gondolas and trams freely, chairs if the player allows it ([[Lift Operations]] step 4).

## Terrain and runs

- **Slow zones and run closures**: the player closes a run or paints a slow zone; guests mostly respect them, and patrollers stand at slow zones slowing fast skiers. *For* [[Alta]].
- **Backcountry**: gates through the boundary to terrain with no patrol or grooming, for experts only. *Needs* [[Land and Boundaries]].
- **Cat skiing**: snowcats carry advanced guests to ungroomed terrain. *For* the Revelstoke idea in [[Scenario Campaign]].
- **Cat trails**: easy, narrow ways down for beginners that get crowded.
- **Glades and bowls as areas**: outlined zones skied anywhere inside, not runs ([[Trail Network]]).
- [[Land and Boundaries]]: a ski area boundary, buying land as a real decision, protected land and buildings. *For* [[Kirkwood]], [[Palisades Tahoe]].
- **Trail steepness for the player**: a run's steepest pitch, "steeper than a green should be" marked on the map, and a fall's cause named.
- **Rock in the sim** ([[Ground Materials]] step 5): avalanches starting above rock bands, guests keeping off rock, experts dropping small cliffs.
- **Biomes**: forested, sub-alpine and alpine zones changing build cost, grooming quality and injury risk.

## Snow and weather

- [[Bough Snow]]: snow on the trees that builds through a storm and lingers after it. *For* [[Asahidake]].
- **Weather and guests**: weather changes who comes and how they feel ([[Demand]], [[Moments]]).
- **Drifts on lee slopes**: the day's wind moves snow off windward faces into lee slopes and gullies.
- **Glaciers**: year-round snow at the top of high resorts. *For* [[Zermatt]].
- **Avalanche control**: explosives, closures, barriers, and an overlay of loaded slopes. *For* [[Alta]].

## Safety

- **Skier-on-skier collisions**: skiers who fail to avoid each other collide and fall, sometimes hurt; more when fast, unskilled or crowded ([[Skiing]]).
- **Clinic**: treats injuries on site instead of sending guests home.
- **Medevac**: a helicopter for serious incidents.

## Guests

- **Splitting up**: the strong skier goes off alone and meets the others for lunch or at the car; "skied with friends", "lost the group" ([[Groups]]).
- **Snowboarders**: their own physics, board and tastes (DLC, [[Release]]).
- **What each skill wants**: beginners want rentals and easy terrain, intermediates food and rest, experts terrain and no crowds. *For* [[Kirkwood]].
- **Rating per skill level**: separate ratings from beginners, intermediates and experts. *For* [[Kirkwood]].
- **Guest goals beyond lapping**: find the shortest line, go to après, stay near the lodge.
- **Regulars and a locals score**: guests who remember their visits, and goodwill that crowds, price rises and heavy building wear down. *For* [[Mad River Glen]].
- **Non-skiing guests**: they come for attractions, food and the village. *Needs* attractions.
- [[Readable Complaints]]: reviews that name the place and why a need went unmet.
- **Guest trip history**: runs taken, vertical and time on the mountain, in the guest popup.

## Base area and services

- [[Services]]: snack stands, a café and coffee, retail, demo and tune shops, lockers, guest services and a pass office; building anywhere, dearer up high.
- [[Service Quality]]: formats at different price points, staff pools per room, a lunch rush and shopping.
- [[Childcare]]: parents ski once their kids are looked after.
- **Views**: well-sited buildings draw more guests and can charge more.
- [[Building Interiors]]: a legend for the cutaway, then furniture.
- **Lodge storeys and styles**: more storeys, a style choice and a shuffle button.
- [[Packed Snow]]: walkers slowed by deep snow, feet packing it down, sweepers clearing stations.

## Staff

- **Employees as people**: they drive in, park in an employee lot and walk to their posts, from a pool per road entry, late or hard to hire when the drive is bad; lift staff arrive and leave.
- **Employee housing**: staff can live on site where commuting is hard. *For* [[Zermatt]].

## Getting there

- [[Large Vehicles]]: buses (shuttles, clubs, school trips) and RVs staying overnight.
- **Trains**: a way in with no parking. *For* [[Zermatt]].
- **Tunnels**: roads and paths through terrain.
- [[Lot Surfaces]]: asphalt, gravel or dirt lots, each its own cost, capacity and look.

## Money

- [[Money Feedback]]: a floating "-$600" when you spend, cost previews on every tool, income and cost flashes on the cash.
- **Demand surveys**: pay for market data on who wanted to come, who stayed home over price, and what would draw more.
- **Loans**: beyond the credit line. *For* the Turnaround idea in [[Scenario Campaign]].

## Real estate and attractions

- **Lodging**: hotels for ongoing revenue, condos and houses sold for land, and a village; on-site beds raise demand. *For* Portillo in [[Scenario Campaign]].
- **Attractions**: an ice rink, a sledding hill, snowmobile tours, nordic trails.

## Campaign

- **Campaign scenarios**: the rest of the [[Scenario Campaign]], each pulling in what its note needs.

## Look

- **Environmental variety**: tree species, deciduous trees, snags, saplings, shrubs and rocks.

## Easter eggs

Things that happen on their own when conditions are right:

- **Pond skimming**: a warm day, a flat runout and a puddle; an expert crosses it and a crowd gathers.
- **Send it**: a pro drops a small cliff and stomps it, or yard-sales and needs [[Ski Patrol]].
- **Bootpacking**: a rested pro hikes above the top lift to an untouched face, and others follow.
- **Mogul show-off**: an expert bombing a mogul run under a lift, entertaining the riders above.
- **Gaper Day**: on the last weekend of the season some guests turn up in retro outfits.
- **Yeti sighting**: very rare, in alpine terrain with low traffic and heavy snow.
- [[SkiFree Mode]], the rest: timed runs with a ghost, and the yeti past the boundary
